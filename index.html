Guest Information

A few helpful details for guests travelling to celebrate with us.

Overnight Stay

Accommodation arrangements will be coordinated by the family. Hotel name and check-in details will be shared directly with the guests concerned.

Mini-Bus Shuttle

A mini-bus shuttle will operate between the banquet hall and the hotel for guests and their luggage. Please coordinate with the family/host team for the pickup point and timings.

Dress & Comfort

Traditional attire is welcome for the wedding rituals. For the Haldi ceremony, please wear shades of yellow. Comfortable footwear is recommended.

Need Assistance?

Please contact the family directly for accommodation, transport, accessibility or any other guest arrangements.
